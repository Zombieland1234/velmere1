# Velmère Cross-Audit Validation and Public Trust Program

## Purpose

Velmère may verify contracts and releases that were previously reviewed by professional auditors, but it must do so only within an authorized contest, active bug-bounty scope, or written project authorization. The program is designed to produce externally verifiable evidence rather than self-awarded certification.

## Mandatory blind protocol

For each of 20 projects, freeze the exact source/commit, chain, address, block, compiler settings, deployed-bytecode digest and Velmère findings before reading the prior audit findings. After the freeze, compare finding-by-finding and preserve accepted, duplicate, invalid, disputed and missed classifications. Publish denominator-based false-positive, duplicate, severity and evidence-coverage metrics.

A public claim that Velmère found vulnerabilities missed in prior professional audits is blocked until at least five Medium/High findings are externally accepted, three are confirmed remediated and three pass a bound retest. One exceptional case is evidence, not a general superiority claim.

## Responsible disclosure

Do not test exploit impact on mainnet, move funds, disrupt services, access secrets or personal data, or publish unpatched details. Use a local fork or authorized test environment. Follow the exact program scope, safe-harbor terms and embargo. Safe harbor is specific to the program and is not permission to test unrelated systems.

## Public trust website

The site must provide a PL/EN/DE trust center, methodology, exact audit registry, responsible-disclosure policy, findings and retest history, signed acknowledgements, independent adjudication/conflicts, benchmark denominators, Basic/Pro/Advanced scope comparison, hash-bound report downloads, release provenance/SBOM/transparency log, provider-rights/freshness status, legal/advice boundaries, incident corrections and WCAG/EAA evidence.

Every public claim must link to exact evidence. Corrections and superseded reports remain visible. Unverified severity, fake certification, private data, credentials and unpatched exploit steps are forbidden.

## Certificate boundary

Velmère may issue its own verification and retest reports, but these are not accredited or third-party certificates. External contest rankings, accepted findings, bounty acknowledgements and signed project letters can be displayed only with exact source references. “Ethereum certified auditor”, “officially certified” or similar claims remain prohibited without a genuine issuing authority.

## Cost boundary

Much of the engineering, public-contest participation, local-fork reproduction and public evidence registry can be performed without an upfront fee. Formal certification, legal/DPO signoff, independent assurance, provider rights, qualified human review and scaled operations are external gates and are not honestly guaranteed to be free.
