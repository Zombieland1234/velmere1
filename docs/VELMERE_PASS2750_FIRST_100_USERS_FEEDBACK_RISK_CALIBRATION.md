# PASS2750 — First 100 Users Feedback and Risk Calibration

## Goal
The first 100 users are not only marketing. They are a calibration layer for product clarity, risk explanation, support states and AI claim discipline.

## Cohort split
- 25 users: Basic only, no account payment.
- 25 users: Pro interest / PDF preview intent.
- 20 users: Advanced/human-review expectation test.
- 15 users: mobile-first testers at 390x844 class viewport.
- 15 users: crypto/market-aware testers who can judge risk copy.

## Feedback categories
- Basic clarity under 30 seconds.
- Pro value before payment.
- Advanced expectation clarity.
- Missing evidence usefulness.
- Risk score vs confidence understanding.
- PDF locked/pending/refunded clarity.
- Angel context/evidence quality.
- Mobile CTA reachability.
- Visual premium feel.
- Trust center/legal wording.

## Daily risk calibration review
Do not change formula silently. Every formula/threshold change must have:
- version,
- reason,
- before/after fixture result,
- risk score delta,
- confidence delta,
- affected lanes,
- rollback path.

## Hard rule
No customer quote, raw email, support ticket, wallet, payment id, IP address, internal note or personal data goes to public release board.
