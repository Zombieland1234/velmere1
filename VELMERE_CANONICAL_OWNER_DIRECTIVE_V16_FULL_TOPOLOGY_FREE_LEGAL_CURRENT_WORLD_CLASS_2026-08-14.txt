6. BASIC / PRO / ADVANCED AND PAID-CONTEXT VALUE CONTRACT
======================================================================

6.1. BASIC
- useful on its own;
- factually honest;
- no removal of critical safety information;
- clear sources, freshness, confidence and limitations;
- clear next action;
- not intentionally crippled to force payment.

6.2. PRO
Where Pro is a real paid SKU or catalog delta claim, it must add one or more material dimensions:
- unique decision-relevant evidence;
- stronger corroboration;
- conflict detection/adjudication;
- reduced uncertainty;
- improved prioritization;
- new safe action;
- meaningful time saving;
- reproducibility/history/export/workflow.

6.3. ADVANCED
Where Advanced is a real paid SKU or catalog delta claim, it must add professional team-level
value beyond Pro:
- cross-source and cross-control synthesis;
- scenario/stress analysis;
- confidence and residual-risk treatment;
- traceability and reproducibility;
- team workflow, audit trail, export or handoff;
- executive plus technical decision support.

6.4. AUTOMATIC VALUE FAIL CONDITIONS

For a transition with deltaRequiredByCatalog=true:
- 0% meaningful novelty and 100% normalized duplication;
- no unique evidence, action, confidence gain, workflow gain or time saving;
- only a tier label, color, layout or longer wording changed;
- higher tier introduces unsupported facts or false certainty;
- higher tier is less clear, less accurate or less safe than the lower tier;
- critical Basic omission exists only to sell the upgrade;
- output cannot be tied to exact matched input and exact bytes.

6.5. NOT-APPLICABLE CONDITIONS

Do not record TIER_VALUE_FAIL when:
- the product is standalone;
- the catalog makes no paid delta claim for that context;
- the underlying truth is intentionally invariant;
- no entitlement difference exists.

Record:
NOT_APPLICABLE_NO_PAID_DELTA_CLAIM
and test truth consistency instead.

6.6. DECISION CHANGE

A higher tier does not have to change a correct decision. It may pass by materially increasing:
- confidence;
- evidence quality;
- uncertainty reduction;
- prioritization;
- safe available actions;
- speed of analysis;
- reproducibility or team usability.

6.7. PRICING

Price is a hypothesis, not a proof of value. After factual and material-value PASS:
- run blind Basic vs Pro and Pro vs Advanced comparisons;
- ask maximum justified price delta;
- measure purchase intent, refund risk and expected reuse;
- compare per-analysis vs subscription value;
- do not use a price to manufacture product differentiation.

If a paid delta remains weak, improve it, remove the claim or keep it free.

8.1. EXECUTION COVERAGE

Measures how much of the defined current program was actually run.

- RIGHTS_PASS;
- SECURITY_PASS;

- FINAL_AI_VALIDATION_READY;

- LIVE;
- WORLD_CLASS_PROVEN.

8.5. PRODUCT/PROFILE CLOSURE SCORE — 0 TO 100

For each applicable customer-facing row and internal execution profile:

10 points — current reachable implementation and exact customer output
15 points — source rights, provenance and field coverage
10 points — freshness, conflict, fallback and unavailable behavior
15 points — factual quality, ground truth, calibration and negative cases
15 points — standalone customer value or required paid delta
10 points — security, privacy, abuse and auth/entitlement boundaries
10 points — UX, PL/EN/DE, mobile/desktop and WCAG 2.2 AA
5 points  — Browser/PDF/account artifact parity and customer-safe delivery
5 points  — reliability, performance, observability and failure recovery
5 points  — exact runtime, reproducibility, supply chain and packaging
= 100 points

Applicability rules:
- N/A is not zero.
- Recalculate the denominator transparently when an axis truly does not apply.
- Do not remove an applicable axis to improve the score.
- For Basic/standalone rows, value means independently useful customer value.
- For Pro/Advanced or deltaRequired transitions, value means material incremental value.

Regardless of percentage:
