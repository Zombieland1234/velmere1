PASS36 A70R0 — MULTIPART UPLOAD + PASSIVE ATTACHMENT TRUST-BOUNDARY HARDENING

- Adds one strict multipart/form-data and passive-attachment boundary for the public contact route.
- Rejects unknown or duplicate fields, mixed File/string field types, malformed boundaries, bidi/control filenames and excessive field values.
- Validates passive PDF EOF and dangerous action names, PNG CRC/chunk order/dimensions/exact IEND, and JPEG segment bounds/dimensions/exact EOI.
- Moves durable idempotency reservation after complete form and attachment validation so invalid uploads do not consume a valid request key.
- Does not claim antivirus, CDR, real Resend delivery, staging, LIVE or sale readiness.
