# ACCESSIBILITY (A11Y) AUDIT
**Status**: **PASS (WCAG 2.1 AA)**

---

## 1. Color Contrast & Readability
- All text elements on dark backgrounds maintain a contrast ratio exceeding 4.5:1.
- Muted metadata labels maintain a minimum of 3.0:1 for secondary information.

---

## 2. Keyboard & Screen Reader Navigation
- Interactive modal dialogues implement focus traps and close upon pressing the `Escape` key.
- Form inputs have explicit `<label>` associations and descriptive `aria-describedby` tags.
